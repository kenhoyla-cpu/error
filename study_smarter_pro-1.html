EARLY ACCESS · PROTOTYPE

Study Smarter — Your Personal AI Study Coach

A student-friendly toolkit for clearer explanations, better practice, and more organized study sessions.

Understand concepts

Break complex topics into plain-language explanations, examples, and checks for understanding.

Practice actively

Build quizzes, flashcards, and teach-it-back exercises from your own course material.

Stay organized

Break assignments into smaller steps and keep track of your study tasks.

Who it is for

College students who want a practical starting point for using AI responsibly as a study aid.

What it is not

This prototype is not a teacher, a guarantee of grades, or a replacement for official course materials. It does not automatically submit assignments or verify AI answers.

This page is a prototype, not a live store. No payment is collected here. Validate usefulness with a few students before setting a price or advertising results.
